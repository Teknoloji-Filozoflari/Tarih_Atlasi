The global GeoNames dump has NOT been downloaded or bundled in this workspace yet.
The optional pipeline selects contemporary settlement and ADM1-ADM4 representative
points; joins administrative names by source codes; preserves source names and
coordinates; splits records by spatial cell/LOD. No historical validity, district
polygons, guessed Wikipedia URLs, machine translation or exhaustive inventory.
Data is provided as is without completeness, accuracy or timeliness warranty.

When installed, source receipts, SHA256, dates, exact coverage and snapshot-specific
NOTICE.txt are in src/places/generated/world. Attribution stays accessible even
with the layer hidden. GeoNames CC BY4.0 is separate from MIT code, Wikidata CC0
and Wikipedia/Commons article/media rights.
