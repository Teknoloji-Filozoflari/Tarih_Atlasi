Tarih Atlası pilot — source and rights

U.S. National Archives and Records Administration (NARA), General Records of the
United States Government, Record Group 11, Treaty of Cession (March 30, 1867),
Article I / IV and proclamation (exchange of ratifications June 20, 1867).
https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/check-for-the-purchase-of-alaska

This package contains a structured editorial extraction of treaty facts and a
project-derived idealized clip of the 141st west meridian between 65 and 68 N.
It does not contain an archived HTTP response, photograph, scan, seal, third-party
GIS polygon, or a claim of a surveyed WGS84 boundary. Clip endpoints are display
limits, not historical border endpoints. Only the 1867-06-20 snapshot is admitted.

Data: CC0 1.0 Universal; factual extraction / idealized derivation.
NARA federal-work reuse policy (not blanket permission for all archival holdings):
https://www.archives.gov/global-pages/privacy.html#copyright
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The source record, extraction SHA, derivation and narrower rights approval are
preserved in the pilot catalog and source manifest. No endorsement by NARA is implied.
Project code remains MIT; these data rights do not change third-party rights.
